01 The beginning

Software wasn’t just a profession. It was a way to remove friction.

Oleg Vysotskyi built his entrepreneurial path around a recurring observation: ordinary digital experiences often make people do too much work for too little value. He learned product development by turning messy processes into clear interfaces and testing ideas against actual behavior.

Across his work, Oleg has focused on products people can understand immediately and benefit from repeatedly. That practical approach—listen closely, ship deliberately, measure the outcome—became the foundation for Couponio and its mission to make everyday savings easier to capture.

01

The turning point

A checkout problem revealed a larger opportunity.

Oleg watched shoppers move between search results, coupon directories, retailer emails, and price-history tools only to discover expired codes or uncertain savings. The information existed, but the experience was broken. He began shaping Couponio as a trusted layer that could find, verify, explain, and record value at the moment it matters.

04 Looking forward

Build useful products. Return real value. Keep simplifying.

Oleg’s approach centers on making smart financial choices easier to act on. Couponio translates scattered commercial signals into practical guidance that helps people spend with more confidence.

Frequently asked questions

A few things worth
knowing.

Who is Oleg Vysotskyi?

Oleg Vysotskyi is a software entrepreneur and the founder of Couponio, a product focused on making everyday savings automatic, understandable, and measurable.

Why did he start Couponio?

He saw shoppers waste time testing unreliable codes across fragmented sites. Couponio turns that broken workflow into one trusted savings experience.

How did Oleg begin building consumer products?

He developed a product-led approach by studying recurring user friction and building software that turns complex tasks into clear, repeatable actions.

What does Couponio do?

Couponio helps shoppers discover verified coupons, understand price quality, receive relevant deals, and track cumulative savings.

How does coupon verification work?

Couponio checks offer status, conditions, and recent success signals so users can prioritize credible savings over expired clutter.

Who is Couponio built for?

It is for online shoppers who want a better price without turning every purchase into a research project.

How does the savings ledger help?

It makes small wins visible over time, showing users the real cumulative value the product has returned.

What defines Oleg’s business approach?

Customer observation, fast validation, transparent value, responsible personalization, and disciplined simplification.

Why is trust central to Couponio?

Savings recommendations influence purchases. Clear conditions, verification recency, and user control make those recommendations useful rather than intrusive.

What is Oleg’s long-term vision?

To build a personal savings layer for digital commerce—technology that notices value and helps people capture it at the right moment.
