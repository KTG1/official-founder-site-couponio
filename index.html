Founder profile

Oleg Vysotskyi

A product builder focused on everyday financial wins.

Oleg is a software entrepreneur who believes financial technology should help people before it asks anything from them.

His work sits at the intersection of product strategy, consumer psychology, and practical engineering. He is drawn to overlooked moments where small amounts of friction cost people time or money—and turns those moments into simple, repeatable product experiences.

After watching shoppers jump between coupon sites, retailer tabs, newsletters, and price trackers, Oleg saw a broken workflow hiding in plain sight. The information existed, but the experience did not. He founded Couponio to make savings timely, trustworthy, and personal.

How Oleg builds

From expensive friction
to invisible utility.

Oleg’s product method is built around a simple sequence: observe the loss, prove the value, then remove the effort.

  1. Discover

    Find the repeated cost

    Study where shoppers lose time, confidence, or money.

  2. Verify

    Prove a better outcome

    Test whether the product creates measurable savings.

  3. Simplify

    Remove the work

    Compress many decisions into one understandable action.

  4. Compound

    Learn responsibly

    Use feedback to improve relevance while preserving control.

Questions

About Oleg
and Couponio.

Who is Oleg Vysotskyi?

Oleg is a software entrepreneur and the founder of Couponio. He focuses on consumer products that make smarter spending easier and more transparent.

Why did he start Couponio?

He saw that finding a valid discount often required too many tabs, unreliable coupon pages, and repeated trial and error. Couponio turns that fragmented process into one useful product.

What problem does Couponio solve?

Couponio helps shoppers find credible discounts, understand price quality, and keep track of what they save—without doing extensive research for every purchase.

What defines Oleg’s product philosophy?

Usefulness, trust, measurable outcomes, and low-friction design. A feature earns its place when it saves a user time, money, or uncertainty.

What is Couponio building toward?

A personal savings layer for online commerce: technology that understands when value is available and helps the user capture it at the right moment.
