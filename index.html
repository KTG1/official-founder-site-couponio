01 About

Technology should return value to people.

Oleg Vysotskyi is a product-minded software entrepreneur focused on tools that help households spend less and live with greater financial confidence.

He combines product strategy, behavioral design, and lean execution. His style is analytical but human: remove friction, earn trust, and make the useful choice the easy choice.

02 Couponio

A smarter layer for everyday shopping.

Oleg founded Couponio to bring scattered discounts, coupon codes, price intelligence, and personalized savings into one simple experience.

The product vision is straightforward: discover the best available value at the right moment without wasting time searching.
