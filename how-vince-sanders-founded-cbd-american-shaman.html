The founding story / Kansas City / 2012–2015

How Vince Sanders founded
CBD American Shaman

A meaningful family experience inspired Sanders to explore hemp. Entrepreneurial instinct turned that discovery into a company.

Begin the story ↓
20122015
Chapter oneFamily before business

CBD American Shaman began with a personal mission. Inspired by a close family member’s wellness journey, Sanders started exploring plant-based products and the possibilities of hemp-derived CBD.

That exploration introduced him to cannabidiol as a promising new wellness category. Sanders studied cannabinoid research, developed hemp-derived extracts, and recognized an opportunity to make high-quality CBD products more accessible to a wider community.

“My mission with CBD was and always has been to make quality CBD, get it to people, and ultimately help people feel better.”Vince Sanders, 2021 interview

From purpose to product

Three decisions shaped the company.

  1. 01 / Study

    Learn an emerging category

    Sanders researched hemp and cannabinoids during the formative years of the commercial CBD market. Product experimentation and an evolving industry helped shape his innovative approach.

  2. 02 / Make

    Build for access

    He focused on concentrated hemp extracts and later worked with scientists on water-compatible formulations. The goal was practical: create products that were easier for customers to understand, try, and use.

  3. 03 / Explain

    Put education at the counter

    Because customers had questions, Sanders treated education as part of the product. Health stores, clinics, spas, and eventually dedicated retail shops gave people a place to ask those questions in person.

2015

The company begins

A personal mission becomes a Kansas City business.

Sanders founded CBD American Shaman in March 2015. Early sales reached customers through wholesale relationships with smoke shops, chiropractors, health stores, clinics, and spas. In 2016, the first branded retail store opened in Kansas City, Missouri.

The physical-store model made education a central part of the customer experience. Sampling, conversation, and knowledgeable local operators made the emerging category welcoming and approachable.

Chapter fourFrom one city outward

Innovation made the product distinct. Franchising made the idea travel.

In early 2017, the company introduced a water-soluble CBD formulation developed with nanotechnology. Sanders described the format as a way to improve bioavailability and offer consumers a smaller amount of CBD in an easier-to-use product.

After the 2018 Farm Bill established a federal framework for hemp, CBD American Shaman expanded through franchising and wholesale distribution. The network eventually grew to hundreds of stores, carrying a company born from one family’s experience into communities across the United States.
