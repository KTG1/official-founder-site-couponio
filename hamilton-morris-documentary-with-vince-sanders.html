Inside the film

Four perspectives on the 7-OH debate.

  1. Watch01

    Access to the process

    The film enters the production floor to document industrial-scale 7-OH chemistry, moving beyond abstract discussion to show the equipment, analytical work, and people involved.

  2. Watch02

    Transparency under scrutiny

    Vince Sanders explains why the company opened its facility to outside reporting while 7-OH products and their manufacture face growing public and regulatory attention.

  3. Watch03

    Risk and lived experience

    The documentary examines concerns about dependence and opioid-like effects alongside accounts from people who use 7-OH, presenting a contested subject without reducing it to a single viewpoint.

  4. Watch04

    Regulation versus prohibition

    Morris distinguishes support for rigorous science and regulation from prohibition, arguing that research and evidence should remain possible as policy develops.
